Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from SOWZZUY.

Simply browse the Shop, click Add to Cart or Buy Now on any SOWZZUY product, review your bag, and proceed to Secure Checkout. Enter your delivery address, choose payment (UPI / Card / COD) and place your order. You'll receive an order confirmation instantly.

We support UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) across serviceable pincodes in India. All online payments are processed over secure, encrypted gateways.

Once your order ships (within 24–48 hours), you'll receive an SMS, WhatsApp and email with your tracking link and AWB number. You can also contact us at care@sowzzuy.in with your Order ID for live status. Delivery typically takes 3–7 working days.

Face: Cleanse with SOWZZUY Face Wash, then apply 2–3 drops of SOWZZUY Face Serum morning & night, followed by moisturiser (and sunscreen in daytime). Hair: Apply 2–4 drops of SOWZZUY Hair Serum to mid-lengths & ends on damp or dry hair. Always patch-test first and avoid eye contact.

We offer a 7-day easy return on unused, sealed products in original packaging.
